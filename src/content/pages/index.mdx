## Aims and scope

This lecture series treats exercise as a medical intervention, integrating current evidence from exercise physiology, metabolic medicine, and immunology to explain how acute bouts and regular training reshape whole-body physiology through signalling between muscle and other organs. The overarching aim is to provide students and practitioners with a mechanistically grounded, clinically applicable understanding of how exercise acts in cardiometabolic and chronic inflammatory disease — and how it can be prescribed, dosed, and adapted to the individual patient.
